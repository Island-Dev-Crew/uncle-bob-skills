# Roster — the Uncle Bob Pack, 50 islands

**Status: HISTORICAL — this is the planning document, kept as written.** All fifty islands were built and shipped (v1.0–v1.2); the tree under [`skills/`](../skills/), not this file, is the inventory. Nothing below has been back-edited to agree with what was built, so where a name or a boundary here differs from the island that shipped, the island is right and this is the record of what was intended. The draft language that follows is left standing for the same reason. Every island cites its evidence: `C‑n` = [`01-CONCEPT-LEDGER.md`](01-CONCEPT-LEDGER.md) (transcript-grounded), `R:x` = [`research/x.md`](../research/), `A‑n` = line-item n of [`03-FORGE50-AUDIT.md`](03-FORGE50-AUDIT.md). Nothing is canon until it survives fusion → gauntlet → registry, per the Forge process. Names are provisional.

**The pack's law**, inherited and extended: *no authority without evidence* — plus the source's native second law: **values transfer; disciplines don't; thresholds move** (C17) — plus its enforcement corollary, Bob's own words: *"You can't tell an agent to be clean. You have to measure the cleanliness that they produce and have them correct failures"* (R:martin-canon).

**Audit rulings honored throughout** (A‑55…75): deep modules is FULLY OCCUPIED — not built; relay skills live inside the serial-role lane spec-pipeline leaves open; gate skills supply METRIC CONTENT and plug into agent-guardrails/archipelago/evidence-packet plumbing, never re-teaching hook wiring, gate infrastructure, or evidence formats; curriculum ships as content through `teach`; the word "gauntlet" is not reused for the human-training island.

## Displacement, versioning, process

- **Count governance:** if the pack is capped at 50, displacement runs through `skill-duel` semantics — never a second swap gate (A‑52).
- **Fusion path (the Forge method, as used for 2.0.x):** Wave 1 fuses first (conversation-native), authored under `idc-skill-authoring` canon with `agents/openai.yaml` sidecars; each island gauntleted against falsifiable EVS with blind critics; Waves 2–3 land as their research is deepened per-island; confidence scores → registry → fleet distribution → v1.x; then cross-model verification + hardening toward v2.x, mirroring the 2.0.3 lineage.
- **Standing corrections to carry:** CRAP creators are Savoia & Evans (not "Alberg"); "Dex Hardy" is Dex Horthy; 100% mutation kill requires the excusal ledger; "agents have huge accurate short-term memory" is Bob's hypothesis, not established research — `threshold-port` treats it as a tunable, not a fact.
